
- installing the rail brackets and the rail as straight and accurately as possible in the elevator shaft
- if rails are not perfectly aligned the elevator can go off the track and ppl can get hurt
- installation begins with spot brackets at top most part of the shaft 
- technicians drop a plumb line to the elevator pit in order to accurately line the lower brackets with the spots
up top
- the plumb line is a steel wire with a 12lb weight at the end to reduce sway over distances
- rail brackets must be perfectly straight levelled and aligned to a tolerance of 1/64 of an inch
- they repeat the installation of the rail brackets from floor to floor and side to side until all the brackets
are in place
- once brackets are in place, guide rails are attached. Guide rails are carefully guided in place with 1 ton
chain hoist, communication is key for safe installation 
- guide choose study the elevator as it travels up and down the shaft. 
- Upward motion is achieved when oil is pumped into the hydraulic pistons causing them to raise 
- The elevator lowers by releasing the oil pressure in the system 
- Sensors monitor and ensure the pistons on both sides of the shaft are moving in sync 
- The selector is a device that monitors floor position. It reads carefully position magnets on the selected tape 
  and commands to stop in a precise alignment with the floor
- During this phase, the computerized motion control system is installed
- Control jump is the bypass safety devices allow the installation of a temporary run box, to move the platform
while on the construction
- Car sling installation constitutes: platform, styles, cross heads and bolts the channel. Its rugged steel 
construction is the foundation on which the rest of the cabin passengers will ride 
*BASIC ELEVATOR TEMPLATE IS NOW IN PLACE

- The rails and brackets are the foundatino of the elevator installation, if the brackets arent on properly the 
rails will be off, and every other item in the entire installation is set to the rail distance. Those items will
be off also.
- The brackets lead us into the rails and the rest of the installation
- Car sling comes in pieces and has to be put on both square and plumb in order for ppl to connect it to the 
previously installed pistons and rails

- Once rails,platform and sling are installed, u can now start setting the entrances (doors u walk through when
u enter the elevator), then they install the cab, then there will be electrical work and programming of the 
elevator in order to get us to permanent operation
- ppl that work in installing the elevator: Masons, steelworkers, steamfitters, fire service, electricians
CAB ASSEMBLY

- As installation continues precise measurements are taken to install the struts
- Struts guide the hold or installation at each landing *EXTENSION* 
- Brackets are installed to hold the struts securely in place
- When completed a full-speed elevator will pass the inches at less than an inch, so they must be installed 
perfectly to avoid a collision *EXTENSION*
- Imperfect struts lead to poorly installed entrances and doors that do not work *EXTENSION*
- The hoist way sill is placed at the entrance on each level
- The hoist way sill and the cap sill must be in perfect alignment *EXTENSION*
- Marks are made to prepare for the installation of the door box 
- A header is placed along the top of the entrance at each landing completing the frame of the door which can now
be installed
- it is secured both top and bottom as always to strict and exact specification. Now its time to install the 
landing door
- the door rides on the landing header resting on smooth rolling casters, it is kept on the track with the hoist
sill using guides known as gibbs *EXTENSION*
- know how perfectly level and aligned the header and hoist sill are, the door hangs and moves with ease *EXTENSION*
- the assembly of the cab begins by loosely joining the side and rear interior walls as they sit on top of the
platform 
- next the team unpacks the dome and ceiling unit to finish off the top of the elevator. These components contain
electronics that will be wired into the rest of the system later to function properly *EXTENSION*
- before the dome is attached, the strike column and return column are installed and the front panels of the car
are assembled.
- This includes the car operating station that contains the cars' vital electronic command and control assembly *EXTENSION*
- It houses a computer interface, auxilary control key switches, fire service operation and call registration
- Once those are in place, the dome is attached *EXTENSION*
- The door control and motor drive unit resides atop the front of the cab and is joined to the front walls and 
the dome to structurally tie all panels together


- With cab assembly complete, all parts are tightened, double checked for square and anchored to the platform in
their final positions
- The cab door is now moved into place, attached to the door operator and adjustments are made until the 
door is running smoothly

DOOR ASSEMBLY
- Gibbs will keep the cab door aligned with the cab sill
- the door clutch assembly is attached to the cab door and adjusted 
- as the cab moves between floors, the clutch will engage the outer doors on each landing and cause them to 
open and close in sync with the cab door when driven by the door operator *EXTENSION*
- the mechanism to lock and unlock the door at each floor is installed and is adjusted for proper operation
- sensors to re-open the door when instructed are installed the electrical wiring on top of the elevator for the 
lights and ventilation fan is complete

ELECTRIC ASSEMBLY
- there are 2 types of wiring involved in elevator installation: the higher voltage electrical wiring that powers
the various electrical systems, and the complex low voltage signal wiring that runs between the computer and 
the various devices and sensors *EXTENSION*
- Wiring of the computer and control systems runs along a cable comprised of hundreds of wires and completing these
connections now make up the bulk of whats left on the job *EXTENSION*
- the crew will field wire all the remaining car control systems *EXTENSION*
- remove some of the jumpers that override computer control and finally remove the run box that has been 
controlling the elevator for most of this installation
- the crew now controls the elevator from the inspection station on top of the car *EXTENSION*
- after controller wiring is completed 1 team member will control the elevator from the control room while the
other keeps track of elevator speeds *EXTENSION*
- they fine-tune the speeds by adjusting the valves which control the rate at which hydraulic fluid is allowed
into and out of the pistons, until the speed is correct *EXTENSION*
- remainder of field wiring for elevator control systems is completed, every button, switch and endpoint in the
system is now connected
- Wires fixed to the non-moving systems are carried through solid conduit (metal pipe), wires that move with the 
car are carried via travelling cable *EXTENSION*
- Once the wiring is completed, the computer is programmed with various command and control protocols required for
the proper operations, the elevator is turned over to full automatic control
- finally every button, switch and system on this elevator install is tested to make sure it is functioning
properly to code *EXTENSION*
- door safety sensors are checked to make sure they open if the light curtain is interrupted (door opens again
if u put hand in the middle) *EXTENSION*
- fire safety systems are checked to make sure that the elevator recalls to the proper floors in case of an 
emergency *EXTENSION*
- once all this is confirmed and any errors are corrected, the elevator is essentially completed, all thats left 
is some tidying up and painting to complete a professional installation

BEFORE THE ELEVATOR IS OPEN TO USE
